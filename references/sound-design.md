# Contextual SFX, no music

Music is prohibited in every layer: source/stock audio, templates, melodic risers/chimes, instrumental nasheed, hum/choir beds and tonal sequences sold as SFX. Audition actual assets/final mix; names/categories are not proof. Preserve clear Google narration or permitted source speech. No local generative sound/speech model is needed.

## Match the action

- Azab/warnings: restrained wind/space ambience or distant thunder when relevant; no unsupported literal unseen sounds or unrelated horror spectacle.
- Desert: wind/sand and actual useful footsteps, never claimed historical recordings.
- Mosque/interior: soft room/door/page/cloth sounds if they help; leave space around quoted meaning and solemn pauses.
- Home/city: relevant door/water/steps/object foley anchored to visible action.
- River/nature: quiet water/wind matching the actual scene.

No fixed effects quota, sound on every word/cut or background music. Silence is valid. The new skill has six background profiles, not the parent's 15 SFX styles.

## Plan and acquire

Plan cue ID, scene/action, purpose and event/word/segment anchor before approval; leave real times/assets unresolved. Copy `assets/sfx-cues.template.json` to `09_edit/sfx-cues.json`; music_allowed stays false.

After workflow guard passes, reuse appropriate known-rights assets or inspect selected [Mixkit SFX](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/) / [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/). Check actual [Mixkit SFX terms](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/), [Pixabay license](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) and asset terms. Record creator/title, page/license, attribution, dates, file hash/properties and actual no-music audition. Number originals under `09_edit/sfx/` and preserve receipts. Do not redistribute standalone stock when prohibited; provide final mix and re-download information.

Resolve actual cue attacks against final voice and animation timing. Use deliberate trim/gain/short fades, sparse overlaps and speech priority. B-roll embedded audio is muted. Main source speech with music needs a real supported removal/replacement plan and listening review; never claim separation from a job status. If required speech cannot be retained cleanly, keep source and report blocker or obtain approval for online Google replacement narration.

Use separate controlled tracks or one premixed master, never both the master and its components. Skip automatic music/voice in all presets. Re-time cues after cuts, voice or animation changes; preserve original recordings.

## Review

Listen to source/assets and final encode for music/melody, meaningful sync, clean tails/joins, clear speech and no duplicates/clipping. Review long ambience too. Waveforms, transcripts and metadata are not a listening pass. Record real inspected intervals/cues, omissions and measurements. Set audio_design.review_status and policy_review.no_music only after actual review, otherwise pending with the limitation stated.
