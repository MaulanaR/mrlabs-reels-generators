## Apply to Islamic content

Build a coherent world suited to the approved discussion, with foreground/midground/background and solid topic objects. Move the camera and objects to explain the narration. For learning/source checking, use a plausible reading room, shelves, desk and an editable source callout; for a journey, a route in a terrain/world, not a floating wireframe globe. Do not make every subject a book scene, and do not copy traffic facts, brand labels, UI, numbers or the reference's topic.

Keep explanatory overlays small and readable. The environment/action is the main image; a big heading, SVG icon and blank colored canvas is not a substitute. Preserve no faces, symbolic prophet markers only, no music, verified sources and user-approved narration/storyboard.

Use actual Three.js or another explicitly approved true-3D path inside the HyperFrames workflow. Validate capture before long production. An unavailable renderer is a reported blocker or a reviewed change of approach; never silently downgrade the accepted look to flat SVG diagrams and mark visual quality passed.
