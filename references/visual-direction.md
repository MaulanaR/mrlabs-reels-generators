Generated B-roll helps when real-world objects, places, actions, or atmosphere carry meaning. Do not force terminals/dashboards into unrelated themes.

## Writing and pacing

- One specific hook in the first roughly two seconds: problem, question, or transformation. Avoid hype the body cannot support.
- A useful 35-second outline: 0-2 s hook, 2-7 s context/analogy, 7-23 s mechanism/examples, 23-31 s payoff/limitation, 31-35 s closing. Adapt to actual narration; these are planning ranges.
- Usually 4-7 main beats. Reveal meaningful components as relevant words are spoken. Do not force a full cut every two seconds or display all information at the start.
- One primary focal element: key term, accurate diagram, relevant screenshot, or state change. Show comparisons visually instead of narrating them over generic scenery.
- End with the promised answer, useful limitation, or one action. Do not invent offers, comments, links, or proof to imitate a CTA.

## Layout, captions, and motion

At 1080 x 1920, start important content within approximately x=80-930 and y=160-1580, reserving extra right/bottom space for social UI. This is a conservative project guide, not a permanent platform specification. Inspect the final framing. Captions usually sit around y=1220-1480; move them when they collide with hero content or a supplied face.

Start with 80-120 px headlines and 52-72 px captions, adapting to word length and real font width. Labels must remain readable in a phone-size preview; remove detail instead of shrinking desktop screenshots. Prefer 2-5 words/group, at most two lines, and selective keyword emphasis. A single emphatic word may work. Hero labels summarize; captions follow speech.

Use one motif and consistent hierarchy. Emphasize with state changes, focus, connector movement, number interpolation, or small position/scale shifts. Entrances and short motivated transitions follow HyperFrames' contract. Avoid empty intros, excessive spins/zoom, flashes between every sentence, and competing kinetic headlines.

Avoid generic AI footage as filler, static slides with only subtitles, unreadable microtext/code, fake metrics/proof, and generated text baked into video. Do not copy reference logos, watermarks, scripts, presenter likenesses, or distinctive illustrated characters. Recreate the visual grammar with original assets and verify the new result.
